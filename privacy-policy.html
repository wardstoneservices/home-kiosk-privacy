Home Kiosk: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Home Kiosk is a private, single-household app that shows a family calendar, the weather, timers and music controls on a screen at home. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What it accesses

If you sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: view-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It reads the titles, times, locations and calendar names of upcoming events so it can show them on the home screen. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

Where the data goes

Calendar information is fetched from Google to a single computer in the owner's home and shown on its screen. It is held in that computer's memory for a few minutes at a time. It is not sent to any other server, shared with anyone, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

What is stored

The only thing saved on disk is a sign-in token, so the app can keep reading the calendar without asking you to sign in again. It stays on that computer.

Your choices

You can stop all access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing "Home Kiosk", and by deleting the token file from the device. Doing so ends the app's access immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Home Kiosk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
